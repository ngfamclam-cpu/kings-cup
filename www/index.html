    Play on the TV

    Mirror your screen, then turn the phone sideways. The layout switches to a big-screen view.

    • Android + Chromecast / Google TV: swipe down for Quick Settings, tap Cast or Screen cast (Smart View on Samsung).
    • iPhone + Apple TV / AirPlay TV: open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring.
    • Laptop on Chrome: menu ⋮ → Cast… → pick the TV, then press F for fullscreen.

    On a keyboard, Space or Enter draws a card.
